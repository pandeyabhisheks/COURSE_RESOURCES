BME 302 syllabus (AKTU, 2023-24 onwards)

Course outcomes

  • CO1: Understand the application of mass and momentum conservation laws for fluid flows (K2).
  • CO2: Understand the importance of dimensional analysis (K2).
  • CO3: Evaluate the velocity and pressure variations in various types of simple flows (K3).
  • CO4: Mathematically analyze the flow in water pumps and turbines (K3).
  • CO5: Understand the functioning of centrifugal and reciprocating pumps (K2).

Units (8 lectures each)

  • Unit 1. Definition of fluid, Newton’s law of viscosity, units and dimensions, properties of fluids, incompressible flow, Bernoulli’s equation and its applications (Pitot tube, orifice meter, venturi meter).
  • Unit 2. Continuum and free molecular flows, types of flow (steady, uniform, laminar, rotational, compressible, subsonic, subcritical, 1D to 3D), streamlines, continuity equation for 3D and 1D flows.
  • Unit 3. Laminar flow through pipes, resistance to flow, minor losses, pipes in series and parallel, power transmission, siphon, water hammer. Boundary layer over a flat plate, separation and its control, drag and lift.
  • Unit 4. Hydrodynamic thrust of a jet, classification of turbines, Pelton wheel (velocity triangles, power, efficiency, governing), Francis and Kaplan turbines, selection of turbines.
  • Unit 5. Centrifugal pumps (classification, vector diagram, work done, efficiencies, specific speed, cavitation), reciprocating pumps (slip, indicator diagram, air vessels), comparison of the two.

BNC 501 syllabus (AKTU, 2024-25 onwards)

Units

  • Unit 1. Introduction and Basic Information about the Indian Constitution. Meaning of constitution, law and constitutionalism; historical background of the Constituent Assembly; Government of India Act 1935 and Indian Independence Act 1947; enforcement of the Constitution; salient features; the Preamble; Fundamental Rights; Fundamental Duties; Directive Principles of State Policy; parliamentary system; federal system; Centre-State relations; amendment procedure and its historical perspective; emergency provisions (National Emergency, President’s Rule, Financial Emergency); local self-government.
  • Unit 2. Union Executive and State Executive. Powers of Parliament; functions of the Rajya Sabha and Lok Sabha; powers and functions of the President (compared with the US President); powers and functions of the Prime Minister; the judiciary (independence of the Supreme Court, appointment of judges, judicial review, public interest litigation, judicial activism); Lokpal, Lok Ayukta and the Lokpal and Lokayuktas Act 2013; State executive (Governor, Chief Minister, State Cabinet); State Legislature; High Court and subordinate courts.
  • Unit 3. Introduction and Basic Information about the Legal System. Sources of law and court structure (enacted law, common law or case law); the court system in India and foreign courts (district court, consumer forum, tribunals, High Courts, Supreme Court); arbitration as an alternative to the courts; contract law, tort and law at the workplace.
  • Unit 4. Election Provisions, Emergency Provisions and Amendment of the Constitution. Election Commission of India (composition, powers, functions and electoral process); types of emergency (grounds, procedure, duration and effects); amendment of the Constitution (meaning, procedure and limitations).
  • Unit 5. Business Organizations and E-Governance. Sole traders and partnerships; the Companies Act (formation, Memorandum and Articles of Association, prospectus, shares, directors, general meetings, auditors, winding up); e-governance and the role of engineers in it; need for reformed engineering services at the Union and State level; role of IT professionals in the judiciary; alienation and secessionism as hurdles to industrial development.

Course outcomes

  • CO1: Identify and explore the basic features and modalities of the Indian Constitution.
  • CO2: Differentiate and relate the functioning of the Indian parliamentary system at the Centre and State level.
  • CO3: Differentiate different aspects of the Indian legal system and its related bodies.
  • CO4: Discover and apply different laws and regulations related to engineering practices.
  • CO5: Correlate the role of engineers with different organizations and governance models.
